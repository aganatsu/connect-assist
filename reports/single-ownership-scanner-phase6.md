# Single-Ownership Scanner Phase 6

Date: 2026-08-03

## Scope

Phase 6 separates authoritative decision evidence from legacy diagnostics in
the trade-details UI.

## Authoritative View

Zone Story remains visible with its linked Timeframe Evidence, including the
selected unified or standalone route. Direction Verdict and current lifecycle
evidence remain visible outside the diagnostic disclosure.

## Legacy Diagnostics

The following are collapsed by default under `Legacy Diagnostics` and labeled
`Does not authorize`:

- percentage score and factor count
- Tier 1, Tier 2 and Tier 3 summaries
- weighted factor breakdown and impulse credits
- classified legacy gate outcomes
- historical gate pass/fail output

The same component is used for live scan details, position details and stored
signal reasoning.
